How to play

LV.1

Start a session

Choose a Point system, Numerical or Fibonacci, set the highest axis value and share the link.

LV.2

Pull up a chair

Everyone joins with just a name. No accounts, nothing to install.

LV.3

Hands down

Pick a Square. Pick another to move it, or the same one to clear it. Nobody else can see it.

LV.4

The Reveal

The Admin ends the session. Every Square shows who landed there; anyone without a Selection is Abstained.

Why keep votes private?

The first number said out loud tends to become everyone’s number. Private Selections mean each person commits to their own read, and the Reveal puts the disagreement where the team can point at it.

House rules

Live, but privateThe session updates in real time, but nobody sees a Selection until the Reveal.
No sign-upOpen the link and type a name. That’s the whole join.
One link per sessionEvery new session gets its own link. Share it wherever your team talks.
Change your mindPick another Square to move your Selection, or the same one to clear it.
Two axes, one gridTime and Resources share the same Axis values, so the grid is always square.
Nothing is keptSessions live in memory. No accounts, no history.
